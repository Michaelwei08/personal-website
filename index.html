When should a clinical AI agent refuse to act?

A clinical agent can fail dangerously without saying anything false — by acting when the right move was to gather a missing lab, abstain, or escalate. I built a benchmark and a runtime guard that sits outside the model, with a 20-case model evaluation and an earlier interactive browser demo.
